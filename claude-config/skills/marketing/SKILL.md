# Marketing Skill - Product-Led Growth Copywriting

Lead with outcomes, write for the skeptic, and make every word earn its place.

## Copywriting Principles

### Lead with the Outcome, Not the Feature
- Bad: "Our app has a drag-and-drop interface"
- Good: "Ship your landing page in 10 minutes without touching code"

### Write for the Most Skeptical Reader
- Assume they've seen competitors, been burned before, and have limited time
- Anticipate objections and answer them inline
- Use social proof (numbers, named customers, case studies) not just testimonials

### The 4 U's for Headlines
Every headline should be:
1. Useful - offers clear value
2. Urgent - creates reason to act now
3. Unique - differentiates from alternatives
4. Ultra-specific - uses concrete numbers/details

### Hierarchy of Persuasion
1. Problem acknowledgment (you feel their pain)
2. Agitation (what happens if the problem persists)
3. Solution presentation (your product as the path out)
4. Proof (evidence it works)
5. Call to action (one clear next step)

## SEO Analysis

When analyzing or writing for SEO:
- Identify primary keyword (1 per page) and 3-5 secondary keywords
- Primary keyword in: H1, first 100 words, one subheading, meta description
- Target search intent: informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional
- Recommend internal linking opportunities
- Title tag format: `Primary Keyword - Brand Name` (under 60 chars)
- Meta description: benefit-driven, includes CTA, under 155 chars

## Content Strategy

When developing content strategy:
1. Define the audience segment (job title, company size, pain point)
2. Map content to funnel stage: Awareness / Consideration / Decision
3. Choose format based on content type: long-form for SEO, short-form for social, case studies for decision stage
4. Define one primary CTA per piece
5. Plan distribution: owned (email, blog), earned (PR, backlinks), paid (ads)

## Email Copywriting

Subject line rules:
- Under 50 characters for mobile
- No ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, or spam trigger words
- A/B test curiosity vs. benefit-driven subjects

Email structure:
- Line 1: hook (reason they opened was justified)
- Body: one idea only, short paragraphs, no walls of text
- CTA: single button or link, action verb + outcome ("Get my free audit")

## Output Format

For any copy deliverable, provide:
1. Primary version
2. Alternative headline (A/B variant)
3. Brief rationale for word choices
4. Suggested A/B test if applicable
